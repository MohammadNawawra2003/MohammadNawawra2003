## 🟣 About Me

I'm a **Computational Engineer** and **Junior Odoo Developer** based in Bethlehem, Palestine, with a strong foundation in numerical methods, finite element analysis, and software engineering. My background bridges **engineering simulation** and **enterprise software development** — from modeling structural behavior under load with ABAQUS, to building Python-based ERP modules and REST APIs that power real business workflows.

I specialize in **Odoo ERP development** (v16–v19), customizing modules across Inventory, Accounting, CRM, Sales, and Purchase, and integrating Odoo with external systems through secure REST APIs. I also bring a research-driven mindset from my exchange semester at UCLouvain, where I worked on Space-Time Discontinuous Galerkin Methods for Hyperbolic Equations.

I care about clean, maintainable code, automating repetitive business processes, and bridging the gap between technical systems and real-world operational needs.
